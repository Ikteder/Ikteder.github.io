# Decision 0002: Label under-review manuscripts separately

Date: 2026-10-03

## Decision

Count SPARQ as a research output while displaying it in a distinct **submitted manuscript · under review** category. Do not include it in peer-reviewed publication totals.

## Rationale

This makes current research visible to recruiters without implying editorial acceptance or publication. The exact venue and title are useful professional context, while the explicit status preserves an evidence-based research record.
